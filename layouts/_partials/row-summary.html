{{- /* One line of plain text describing a page, for list rows and meta tags. Front-matter `summary` wins. Failing that, Hugo's auto-summary is raw rendered HTML, which is fine for prose but wrong for a reveal-hugo deck: it is slide markup — headings, rules, page-relative srcs that resolve only on the deck's own page (so they 404 on an index), and the contents of
, which are speaker notes and have no business on a public page. Drop the notes, flatten the rest to text. Callers truncate. Two passes, because the auto-summary is a truncated prefix of the page and can end mid-
: the first removes complete note blocks, the second removes a block left hanging open by the truncation. Without the second, speaker notes still reach the page whenever the cut lands inside one. Usage: {{ partial "row-summary.html" . | truncate 180 }} */ -}} {{- $s := .Summary -}} {{- with .Params.summary }}{{ $s = . }}{{ end -}} {{- return ($s | replaceRE `(?s)]*>.*?
` "" | replaceRE `(?s)]*>.*` "" | plainify | htmlUnescape | chomp) -}}
